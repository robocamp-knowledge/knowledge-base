## The challenge is not starting robotics. It is making it continue.

Robotics can work beautifully on its own and still fail to become a program.

That happens when lessons are prepared one by one, when every group starts from a different place, or when equipment is used only when someone has time and confidence to plan around it. The result is familiar: students stay engaged, but progression is unclear. Teachers do good work, but too much depends on individual preparation. Schools invest in equipment, but cannot always show how it supports learning over time.

RoboCamp closes that gap: with structured lessons, teacher support and a pathway that helps robotics become part of regular learning.

## A clear lesson structure teachers and students can follow

A RoboCamp lesson is not only a building instruction. Each activity gives teachers and students a repeatable flow: introduce the challenge, build the model, explore how it works, program it, test it and improve it.

That structure makes lessons easier to run and easier to understand. Teachers know what the lesson is trying to achieve, while students see the connection between the model, the program and the idea behind the task.

### Consider

Introduce the context, challenge or real-world idea behind the project.

### Create

Construct the model step by step with clear visual instructions.

### Check

Explore the mechanism, movement, sensors or concept behind the model.

### Code

Bring the model to life with code, from simple sequences to more advanced logic.

### Celebrate

Test, improve, modify and extend the project.

The value is not only that the robot works. Students understand why it works — and what they can change next.

## A path that grows with your students

Good robotics lessons should not feel like a reset every week.

When every activity stands alone, students may stay engaged, but the learning path becomes harder to see. A stronger robotics program gives students familiar ideas in new forms. Movement becomes sensor control. Simple sequences become decisions and debugging. Guided building becomes more independent engineering work.

RoboCamp courses help organize that growth, from early robotics and basic code to more advanced concepts and extensions.

### Beginner robotics

First experiences with building, sequencing, movement and simple programming.

### Programming foundations

Core coding ideas introduced through physical models: loops, conditions, events, sensors and debugging.

### Intermediate challenges

More complex mechanisms, sensor-based decisions and problem-solving tasks.

### Advanced engineering and coding

Projects that require planning, testing, iteration and more independent student work.

### Python, AI and computer science readiness

A bridge from block-based programming toward text-based code, data, decisions and modern computing concepts.

### STEAM and makerspace expansion

Projects that combine robotics with design, 3D printing, prototyping, laser cutting and creative engineering.

The goal is not to make every lesson harder. The goal is to make each stage connect to what students have already learned.

## Practical questions before you start

### Is RoboCamp only for beginners?

No. RoboCamp supports educators who are just starting, but it is also designed for teachers, instructors and organizations that already run robotics activities and want better structure, progression and learning outcomes.

### Do I need robotics experience?

No. RoboCamp provides step-by-step lesson materials and teacher training. Teachers still need to understand the lesson flow, but they do not need to build the curriculum from scratch.

### What if we already run robotics classes, clubs or FLL preparation? Can RoboCamp help us improve?

Yes. RoboCamp can support both new implementations and existing programs. We can help you organize existing activities into a more structured pathway and build stronger foundations in programming, engineering and STEAM.

### What equipment do we need?

The best course sequence depends on the equipment you already have. We can help you choose materials based on your robotics sets, student age and goals.

### What if we do not have robotics sets yet?

RoboCamp does not sell robotics sets directly, but we can direct you to a distributor if you need hardware together with a RoboCamp plan.

### Can we use RoboCamp with different robotics kits?

Yes. The goal is to help you build a coherent learning pathway from available resources. The right route depends on your equipment and teaching goals.

### Can we combine old and new equipment?

Yes. RoboCamp can help you use existing equipment more effectively while adding new courses, tools or STEAM extensions over time.

### Should we buy one course or choose a plan?

A standalone course is a good focused start if you need one kit, one topic or one level. A Teacher Plan makes more sense when one educator teaches regularly and needs broader access. A School Plan is better when several teachers, classes or groups need a shared program.

### Can you help us choose the right courses?

Yes. That is one of the main reasons to request a quote or consultation. We can recommend a pathway based on your equipment, age groups and teaching goals.

### Is teacher training included?

Yes. Teacher training is included by default, so educators can understand the lesson flow and prepare for real classroom use.

### Do students need personal accounts?

No. RoboCamp uses time-limited lesson access generated by the teacher. These accounts work more like temporary workstation access than personal student profiles. Teachers can manage access for the whole group or separately in real time. This supports student privacy because RoboCamp does not store student data.

### Can RoboCamp support a school-wide implementation?

Yes. RoboCamp can support individual teachers, teaching teams, schools and larger educational organizations.

### Can after-school programs use RoboCamp?

Yes. RoboCamp can support regular robotics, coding and STEAM activities in after-school, enrichment and club settings.

### Can we start small and expand later?

Yes. You can start with a focused course or pathway and expand into additional courses, plans and STEAM content over time.

### Is RoboCamp available for homeschoolers?

Yes. Homeschool access is available, although this page focuses mainly on schools and educational organizations.
